Capture the problem, prepare the prompt, hand it over.

1m: Private Prompt Composer is an iPhone app for developers. Point it at the logs, the code or the error in front of you. It reads the text out of what you captured, looks for what should not leave your phone and covers what it finds for you to review, builds a well-formed prompt around it, and hands the whole package to an AI app you already have — in one share or sequentially, depending on the share mode you choose.

  • No API keyThe app never calls an AI service itself. You send the request from an app you already have on your device.
  • No accountNothing to register and nothing to sign into. The trial and the purchase belong to your Apple Account and are handled by Apple; we never see your Apple Account or your payment method.
  • No serverCapture, text recognition and redaction all run on your iPhone. There is no backend of ours, no analytics and no third-party SDK.
  • You review before you sendKeys, hosts, emails, paths and names the app finds are covered before you share, and every one is shown on screen for you to check. It can only cover text it managed to read, and detection is not exact, so the last look is yours.

Privacy Policy Support

What it costs

A free 3-day Trial is activated with the App Store when you are ready — you start it yourself, it runs for 72 hours from that activation, and there is no automatic charge, no subscription and nothing to cancel. Full Access is a single one-time purchase at the price the App Store shows you, with no time limit; it belongs to your Apple Account, can be restored on another device, and can be shared through Family Sharing. Refunds are requested from Apple, in Apple's own sheet. Activating, buying, redeeming an offer code and Restore purchases each need a connection to the App Store; once your device holds the purchase, it is read back without one.

In short

1m collects nothing. There is no server of ours, no account, no analytics, no third-party SDK and no tracking, and the app has no network code of its own. The only network activity is iOS talking to Apple on the app's behalf: Apple's speech service where your language has no on-device model, Apple's translation assets, and the App Store for the trial, the purchase and Restore. Settings also carries links that open these two pages in your browser, and a Report a problem row that opens your own mail app with a draft you can read, edit or delete before sending — the app sends nothing itself.

Your material stays on your iPhone until you press Share yourself and choose where it goes — at which point the app you picked receives it, under its own privacy policy. The privacy policy spells all of that out.
