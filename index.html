Capture the problem, prepare the prompt, hand it over.

1m is an iPhone app for developers. Point it at the logs, the code or the error in front of you. It reads the text out of what you captured, looks for what should not leave your phone and covers what it finds for you to review, builds a well-formed prompt around it, and hands the whole package to an LLM app you already have — in one share or sequentially, depending on the share mode you choose.

  • No API keyThe app never calls an AI service itself. You send the request from an app you already use on your device.
  • No accountNothing to register, nothing to sign into, no email address to give anybody.
  • No serverCapture, text recognition and redaction all run on your device. There is no backend.
  • You review before you sendKeys, hosts, emails, paths and names the app finds are covered before you share, and every one is shown on screen for you to check. Detection is not exact, so the last look is yours.

Privacy Policy Support

In short

1m collects nothing. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it contains no networking code of its own — the only network activity is iOS talking to Apple on the app's behalf. Your material stays on your iPhone until you press Share yourself and choose where it goes — at which point the app you picked receives it, under its own privacy policy. The privacy policy spells all of that out.
